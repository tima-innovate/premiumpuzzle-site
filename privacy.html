Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy covers the Android apps Multi Games and Multi Party Games published by PremiumPuzzle ("we", "the apps").

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, scores, language choice and statistics are stored locally on your device. We have no servers and no user accounts, and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling an app removes it.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or microphone/camera data. The apps read the device motion sensor (Multi Party Games, to detect tilting) and touch input only while you play, and this never leaves your device.

Advertising and purchases

Where an app shows ads or offers in-app purchases (for example "Remove Ads"), these are provided by third-party services such as Google Play Billing and advertising networks. Those providers may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage information under their own privacy policies. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details. Versions without ads or purchases do not use any of these services.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can uninstall an app at any time to remove all data it stored on your device, and you can reset your Android advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
